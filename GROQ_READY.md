## 🎤 How It Works Now:

1. **Call connects** → AI greets with engaging question
2. **User speaks** → Twilio captures speech
3. **Groq processes** → Generates response with follow-up questions
4. **AI responds** → ElevenLabs TTS speaks the response
5. **Loop continues** → Natural back-and-forth conversation
6. **User presses #** → Call ends gracefully
